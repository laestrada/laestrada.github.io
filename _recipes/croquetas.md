## Ingredients
* Ham, 40% of the total meat weight
* Pollo guisado, 60% of the total meat weight
* Bread crumbs, enough to coat
* Eggs, beaten, enough to coat
* Vegetable oil, enough for shallow-frying

## Preparation:

1. Process the ham and chicken separately into roughly 3 mm pieces, then mix them together.

1. Whisk eggs and put bread crumbs in a bowl.

1. Form the meat into small logs, coat each one in egg, and roll it in bread crumbs.

1. Heat the vegetable oil in a cast-iron skillet. Fry the croquetas until browned, turning so all sides cook evenly.
