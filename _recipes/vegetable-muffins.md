## Details
* Prep Time 15 minutes
* Cook Time 20 minutes
* Total Time 35 minutes
* Servings 12 Muffins

## Ingredients
* 1 medium (250g) Zucchini, grated
* 1 medium (120g) Carrot, grated
* 1/4 cup (30g) Frozen Peas
* 1/4 cup (30g) Frozen Corn
* 1 cup (125g) Cheddar Cheese, grated
* 1/2 cup Milk
* 1/4 cup Plain Greek Yoghurt
* 1/4 cup Olive Oil
* 2 Eggs
* 2 cups (250g) plain wholemeal or all-purpose flour
* 3 tsp Baking Powder

## Preparation

1. Preheat oven to 180C / 350F

1. Place the zucchini and carrot on a clean dish towel, wrap it around the vegetables, and squeeze out as much liquid as possible.

1. Place the zucchini and carrots in a large mixing bowl with the peas and corn. Add the cheese, milk, yogurt, olive oil, and both eggs; stir until combined.

1. Mix the flour and baking powder, then slowly add them to the wet ingredients. Fold gently until just combined; the batter should remain lumpy.

1. Grease a 12-cup muffin pan. Divide the batter evenly among the cups and bake for 20-25 minutes, checking with a skewer near the end. The skewer should come out clean.

### Notes

* Less-processed wholemeal flour may produce a denser muffin; all-purpose flour also works.
* Save the squeezed vegetable liquid for smoothies or pasta sauces.
* Strong cheddar may provide enough salt, but add salt or chopped herbs to taste.
